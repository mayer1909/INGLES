Reglas de 3ra Persona (He / She / It)

Solo en oraciones afirmativas

Regla general (+S) He works / She plays
Termina en -o, -ch, -sh, -ss, -x (+ES) She goes / He watches / He brushes
Consonante + Y (-IES) Maria studies English
Irregular & Negativo (¡Trampa de trivia!) He has breakfast. / She doesn't like tea.
